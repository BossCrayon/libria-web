## ✨ What is Libria?

Libria is built around a simple idea:

> **Reading should feel like reading.**

The app focuses on providing a clean and comfortable place to discover, organize, download, and read novels without turning the reading experience into a monetized obstacle course.

### Reader First

- 📚 Personal library
- 📖 Comfortable novel reader
- 🌙 Multiple reading themes
- 🔤 Adjustable text size and typography
- 📑 Reading progress
- 🔖 Bookmarks
- 📥 Offline reading
- 🔍 Novel discovery and search
- 📱 Designed for mobile reading

---

## 🚫 What Libria Doesn't Do

Libria is intentionally different from many modern reading platforms.

We do not want reading to feel like a game designed to make you wait or pay.

Libria aims to avoid:

- ❌ Intrusive advertisements
- ❌ Chapter paywalls
- ❌ Artificial reading limits
- ❌ Energy systems
- ❌ Waiting timers
- ❌ Forced subscriptions for basic reading
- ❌ Unnecessary notifications
- ❌ Distracting interface elements

The goal is simple:

**Open the app → find your book → continue reading.**

---

## 📚 Your Library

Libria keeps your reading experience centered around your own library.

You can organize and manage the novels you read, keep track of your progress, and return to your books without having to search for them again.

Your library is designed to work even when you are offline.

---

## 📥 Offline Reading

Libria is designed with offline reading in mind.

Downloaded content can remain available when you:

- have no internet connection
- have limited mobile data
- are traveling
- are somewhere with poor connectivity

Internet access may still be required for features such as discovering new content, synchronizing your account, or obtaining new chapters.

---

## 🌐 Discover

Libria can provide a unified discovery experience across supported sources.

Rather than forcing readers to think about where a novel came from, Libria aims to present novels as a single library experience.

A novel may have multiple supported sources while still appearing as **one novel** inside Libria.

---

## 🔐 Your Data

Libria is designed around local-first reading.

Your basic reading experience should not depend on a remote server being available.

Where supported, Libria may synchronize information such as:

- Library contents
- Reading progress
- Bookmarks
- Account information
- Other supported preferences

Some online features naturally require an internet connection.

## 🛡️ Content & Sources

Libria is a reading application and catalog interface.

The availability of novels and other reading material depends on the sources and content that Libria is permitted to use.

Libria does not intend to bypass:

- DRM
- authentication
- paywalls
- CAPTCHAs
- anti-bot systems
- access restrictions
- other technical or legal restrictions

Content should only be provided through sources and methods that permit its use.

If you are a rights holder and believe content is being made available through Libria without appropriate authorization, please contact the project maintainer.

---

## ❤️ Supporting Libria

Libria is intended to remain accessible to readers.

The project does not rely on intrusive advertising to provide the basic reading experience.

If support options become available, they will be used to help maintain things such as:

- Hosting
- Servers
- Storage
- Development
- Infrastructure
- Maintenance

Supporting Libria should never be required simply to read.
